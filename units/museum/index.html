AMBER FLYNN WING TO MYSTICAL GRIMOIRES

Wall text

Yale Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library. The marble tower for rare books.

This wing points at the Mellon Alchemy Collection, a Mary and Paul Mellon gift. About 300 medieval and Renaissance alchemy and occult manuscripts. Newton alchemy notes sit in the same curatorial neighborhood. Mary Mellon began the collecting in the 1940s after reading Jung. The gift reached Beinecke after she died at 41.

Nothing in this room is for sale. The cases are for looking. The outbound door goes to Beinecke Early Books and Manuscripts, where the Mellon Alchemical Collection is described.
